Quick Answers

The stuff everyone asks before riding. StarBot knows the rest.

How old do you have to be to rent?

Riders must be 16+ with a valid ID to operate. Younger riders can ride along with an adult.

Do I need a boating license?

If you were born on or after Jan 1, 1988, Florida requires a Temporary Boating Certificate — you can complete it online in about 20 minutes before you ride.

Is there a security deposit?

Yes, a refundable deposit applies at check-in. No surprises — it's returned when the ski comes back healthy.

Is this guided?

Rides are open-water (your rules within the riding area); Tours follow a certified guide. Both include safety gear.

What if it storms?

Bad Weather Protection: we rebook you or refund you. Your slot isn't lost to a squall.

How many people per ski?

Two riders max per jet ski, within the weight limit. Ask StarBot for details when you book.

Do you take walk-ins?

When we have skis on the dock, yes — but same-day online booking locks your slot so you're not waiting on the sand.

Can we switch riders mid-session?

Yes — come back to the dock and swap riders during your rental.
