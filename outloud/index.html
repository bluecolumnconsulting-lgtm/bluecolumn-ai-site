How does the AI actually work?

Your OutLoud site answers customer questions directly, and if it can't answer something it routes the question to you instantly by phone, email, or SMS — with the full conversation history attached so you know exactly what they asked.

What does setup look like?

Week 1: onboarding. Weeks 2–3: we train the AI on your services, pricing, FAQs, and photos. Week 4: it goes live with monitoring. Most businesses see a 15–40% increase in booked appointments in month one.

Can customers still text, email, or call me directly?

Yes — customers can talk to the AI, text, email, or be routed straight to your phone. All channels flow through one intelligent system, and everything is controlled from a simple dashboard. No technical skills required.

Can I customize how it looks and sounds?

Completely. Choose the avatar (animated character, real person, or voice only), set the tone (professional, friendly, casual), and set priorities — booking, lead qualification, or customer support.

What does it cost?

AlwaysOn Essential is $497 setup + $97/mo. AlwaysOn Lead Engine is $997 setup + $197/mo and adds a managed lead list for your trade and service area. Setup is one-time, no contracts — month to month.

How long until it's live?

About 30 days end to end: onboarding in week one, training in weeks two and three, live with monitoring in week four. We handle the build, run, and management the whole way.
