## What is BlueColumn?

BlueColumn helps AI agents store information and retrieve it later. It provides persistent agent memory — information saved outside a single conversation so an agent can retrieve it in a later session.

## What is OutLoud?

OutLoud is a talking-avatar experience that gives your business a conversational assistant with a spoken, visual presence for website visitors. You teach it about your business through your profile and uploaded information, and customers can ask questions instead of searching your website.

## How They Work Together

BlueColumn provides the memory backbone, while OutLoud gives customers a spoken, visual way to interact with business information. They are related offerings designed to help agents retain useful information and help website visitors get answers from your business's information.

## How OutLoud Works

You add your business details, choose an avatar, and upload your knowledge. Visitors ask questions, and the avatar uses that information to answer aloud. It is designed for live questions and spoken answers rather than prerecorded content. Visitors get a conversation instead of searching: they ask a question, hear an answer, and see the right information appear on screen.

## Who It's For

Businesses wanting visitors to ask about services, prices, and next steps — including small businesses, home services, retail, hospitality, and professional services.

## What the Avatar Can Answer

The avatar answers questions based on information you provide about your business. It works best with:

- **Business services and offerings** — what you do
- **Pricing and costs** — your rates
- **Next steps** — how customers contact you or book
- **Policies and FAQs** — common questions your customers ask

Example customer questions:

- "What services do you offer?"
- "How much does this cost?"
- "How do I book an appointment?"
- "What is your service area?"

## What Information Should I Upload?

1. **Your business profile** — name, phone, email, service area, business description
2. **Suggested questions** — up to four questions that help new visitors understand your offer
3. **Knowledge documents** — FAQ, pricing, policies, service details (check supported formats in your account uploader)

Start with the questions your customers ask most often. The avatar can respond to different wording of questions when the relevant information is available, so you don't need word-for-word matches in your FAQ.
